I know it has been said over and over that you don't appreciate something until it is gone, well recently I haven't had sheets. Now that doesn't mean that I been sleeping on a bare mattress, I have had a sleeping bag, but no sheet. Wow, I would never have thought that a simple piece of material could have such an impact on your sleeping experience and thus there is now a blog post to bring sheets into the spotlight and hopefully bring them some recognition for what they do, an ode to sheets if you will. Be Sleepy
