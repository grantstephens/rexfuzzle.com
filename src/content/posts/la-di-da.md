So there I was skipping and hopping through life and thinking is well. Happiness, Bliss etc etc. Then comes a story about something bad that happened to someone I know. Crisis! Was I that blind. This is the real world and there is a lot of pain and suffering and stuff that goes on. The question is, what to we do about it. Do we keep the La-Di-Da attitude?I just don't know, but a quote from Al Gore seems relevant: "I promise you a day will come when our children and grandchildren will look back and they will ask one of two questions. Either they will ask, 'What in God's name were they doing? Didn't they see the evidence?'" Or, he said, "They may look back and say 'How did they find the uncommon moral courage to rise above..."Be Having Moral Courage
