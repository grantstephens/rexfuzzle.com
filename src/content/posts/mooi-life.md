I look out my window  
Down to the road below  
People all driving in a row  
Life flies by  
Sometimes you sigh  
Just to get by  
The appears a train  
suddenly into the frame  
And you're no longer tame  
Now you're in joy  
It's like having a new toy  
And like is very mooi!
