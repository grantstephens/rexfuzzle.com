So yesterday I played paintball and usually people are scared and stuff. This time, not so much. Paintball is a different game when two people start running straight at each other instead of trying to dodge bullets. I have also decided that when they say pain is temporary but glory lasts forever it is very true, but since glory is well, there is a post coming up about that, but in this case the silly grin lasts at least a day or two. That being said, the pain is still there, but the grin more than make up for it. Another thing to note is that as usual it is the people that make the game, so choose wisely and don't be surprised when you find yourself in a paintball-quick-draw. Be Brave
