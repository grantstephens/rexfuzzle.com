You know those days that just don't go. I can't quite tell you what exactly went wrong cause nothing did specifically but just felt like I was getting nowhere the whole day. One thing after another and I was just like seriously!Then, out of the blue, the blue of Monday, it started going. I solved a problem, had a conversation with someone and everything seemed ok again. Actually more than that, I made the decision, once again, that I will be positive, even on a Monday when things are kinda glum, I will I will I will!So the blue monday turned out to be a cool blue and deep and dark all at the same time.
