So I've been busy building a few things and decided I needed a place to showcase them. [hub13.xyz](https://hub13.xyz) is that place. It is also a domain where I can host some of them so that the most expensive part of these little projects isn't buying a domain every time.

While we're here, let's also do a quick intro to some of the projects that already exist:

- [Wind Co-op Dashboard](https://dashboard.windcoop.co.uk/): live monitoring for a community wind farm, built for the Graig Fatha co-op. This is interesting because it is in TinyGo and runs on the edge- no backend.
- [Gather](https://gather.hub13.xyz): a self-hosted local events aggregator- [Perthshire.Events](https://perthshire.events) runs on it.
- [Lochle](https://lochle.xyz) - guess the Scottish loch, a new one every day. Turns out there are a lot of lochs (over 25000) and so this is insanely hard. Somebody once got it in two.
- [Earn Your Descent](https://earnyourdescent.com): upload your runs and rides and only then can buy some exclusive swag. This is a bit of a life motto one for me- you have to earn things, not everything has to be instant gratification. Also, pushing back a bit on consumerism and even a bit eco as all garments are [Remill fibre](https://remillfibre.com/)
- [Flux](https://flux.hub13.xyz) - a personal finance app that tracks your net money rate in real time, via open banking. This has hit a major road block because open banking isn't actually so open.
- [ReminDiary](https://remindiary.hub13.xyz) - a private journal that remembers what you wrote on this day in years past. This has now been published on [F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/xyz.hub13.remindiary/) (an alternative app store with principles).
- [Ridgeline](https://ridgeline.hub13.xyz): turn any mountain or hill into a custom line-art print. Still working on this, but some of the pictures it produces are very cool!
- [Totpad](https://github.com/grantstephens/totpad) - a fast TOTP authenticator for the Adafruit MacroPad, keyed from a 2FAS backup, in CircuitPython. Use this every day!

More of these to come, but needless to say, I'm having fun!
